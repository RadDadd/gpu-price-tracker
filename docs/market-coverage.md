# Tracked-market coverage and median

The tracker follows exact retail models within GPU-family/VRAM groups. It is a sample of the new-card market at Micro Center Overland Park (store 191), Newegg, Best Buy, and B&H Photo; Amazon and third-party marketplace sellers are excluded. It is not a census of every available GPU listing.

## Initial expansion, November 5, 2026 (Central Time)

Coverage expanded from 7 Gigabyte models / 28 listings to 14 models / 37 listings across Gigabyte, PNY, MSI, and PowerColor. Each of the seven GPU families has two configured models. The additional models are PNY RTX 5060 Ti OC Dual Fan, RTX 5070 OC Triple Fan, RTX 5070 Ti OC Triple Fan, RTX 5080 Slim OC, MSI RTX 5060 VENTUS 2X OC, and PowerColor RX 9060 XT and RX 9070 XT Reaper. The PNY RTX 5070 Ti is configured at Micro Center, Best Buy, and B&H. Other newly added models initially have Micro Center coverage; cross-retailer coverage remains uneven.

Catalog identities, UPC/EANs and basic specifications for new models were verified against their exact retailer product pages. `specs_status: retailer_verified` distinguishes those from manufacturer-verified catalog entries. Retailer part numbers can be abbreviated; UPC/EAN plus retailer SKU provides the matching identity. A failed or unavailable listing is never replaced with another model or seller during a routine check.

## Calculation

For each unique collection run and GPU-family/VRAM/memory-type group, take every successful, fresh, new, in-stock offer from an approved direct retailer. Each exact model/retailer/seller/condition/location identity contributes once. Two different models at one retailer both count; duplicate listings for the same offer do not. Take the middle price for an odd count or the arithmetic mean of the two middle prices for an even count. This is an offer-weighted median, not inventory-, sales-, retailer-, or model-weighted. Shipping and tax are excluded; unknown shipping is not assumed free. Sold-out advertised prices remain in history but are excluded from this purchasable-price statistic.

Show the qualifying offer count, distinct model count, distinct retailer count and price range with each point. Preserve original historical observations; earlier runs genuinely have narrower coverage. Do not backfill new models or carry previous prices into a new run. Break the chart line when contributing model coverage changes. Price, availability and coverage changes can all move this statistic.

Exact-offer price deltas continue to compare only the same stable offer identity. Market medians never replace or rewrite exact-model history. Adding more verified models and retailers improves coverage but does not justify claiming complete-market accuracy.
